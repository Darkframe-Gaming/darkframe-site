LAST UPDATED · JULY 2026

The short version

This website is a static portfolio. It has no accounts, no forms, no analytics scripts, no advertising and it sets no cookies. Fonts and images are served from this domain — your visit triggers no third-party tracking requests from us.

1. Data we collect

None directly. We do not collect, store or process personal data through this website. We run no analytics and no tracking pixels.

2. Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log standard technical data (such as IP addresses) for security and operational purposes when serving pages. That processing is governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Email

If you email us at contact@darkframegaming.com, we receive your address and the contents of your message. We use them only to reply and conduct the correspondence you initiated; we don't add you to mailing lists or share your details with third parties.

4. Our games

Darkframe games are played on third-party operator platforms. Those platforms handle their own accounts, payments and player data under their own privacy policies — this policy covers only this website.

5. Your rights

Since we hold no data about your visit, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For anything related to email correspondence, contact us and we will delete it on request.

6. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated date.
