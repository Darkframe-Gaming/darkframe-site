Responsible Play

LAST UPDATED · JULY 2026

We make slots because we love the craft of them — the art, the math, the suspense of a good bonus. But our games are entertainment with a real cost, not a way to make money. Every Darkframe game is built around a fixed return to player below 100%: over time, the game keeps a share of what is wagered. That is what RTP means.

Play by these rules

  • Play with money you can afford to lose — treat it as the price of entertainment.
  • Set a budget and a time limit before you start, and stop when you hit either.
  • Never chase losses. No streak, pattern or "due win" exists — every round is independent.
  • Don't play when upset, intoxicated or under financial pressure.
  • Use the deposit limits, reality checks and self-exclusion tools your operator provides.

Age restriction

Our games are strictly for adults: 18+, or higher where local law requires. If you share a device with minors, use the parental-control tools of your platform.

If it stops being fun

If gambling is causing you or someone close to you harm, free and confidential help is available worldwide:

  • BeGambleAware — advice, tools and live support
  • Gambling Therapy — international, multi-language support
  • Gamblers Anonymous — peer support meetings worldwide
  • Gamban / GamBlock — software that blocks gambling sites and apps

A note on our design

We publish honest numbers — RTP, maximum win and volatility — for every game, and we never design mechanics to disguise losses as wins or to pressure you into continuing. The house edge is printed on the tin. Please read it.
