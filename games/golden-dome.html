THE FEATURES

How it plays

Nothing in this game resets. Meters, markers and multipliers only ever climb.

01 PAY ANYWHERE · 8+

Forget lines and clusters — only the count matters. Eight or more of a symbol anywhere across the 35 cells burst together, in three tiers: 8–10, 11–13 and 14+. The displayed paytable is the win function, exactly.

02 STICKY ×BOMBS

Bombs only ever drop after symbols burst — never onto a dead board. Values run ×2 through ×100, they stack on a cell, and inside DEFCON every bomb leaves a permanent marker: the feature multiplier is one plus every bomb dropped so far, so it can only grow.

03 ARMED COLUMNS & AIRSTRIKE

Every cell that bursts ticks its own column's meter, and those meters never reset for the rest of the feature. Ten ticks arm a column — and the moment a bomb lands in an armed column, the entire column turns wild.

04 DEFCON FREE SPINS

Three, four or five sirens open 8, 11 or 14 free spins — and the entry board's own bursts already resolve inside the feature, charging meters and paying at the feature multiplier. More sirens during the round add five spins, up to thirty.
