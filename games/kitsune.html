THE FEATURES

How it plays

Every tail is a step up. Every strike makes the board richer.

01 KITSUNE WILD

The fox spirit fills its entire reel and substitutes on all ten lines it crosses.

02 TAIL MULTIPLIERS

Each tail on the Kitsune is a rung on a ladder from ×2 to ×500 — the more tails, the bigger every line it touches.

03 SYMBOL STRIKE

When a Kitsune lands it strikes, turning one low letter symbol into a premium across the board before the lines pay.

04 STICKY FREE SPINS

Three, four or five scatters open 10 free spins in which every Kitsune sticks with its multiplier until the round ends.
