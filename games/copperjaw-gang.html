THE FEATURES

How it plays

Wilds that multiply, a bounty that collects, and a posse that sticks around.

01 MULTIPLIER WILDS

Every Wild lands carrying a multiplier, and all the multipliers on one line multiply together.

02 BOUNTY HUNT

When three or more Wilds land, the biggest one collects the bounty — taking the sum of every Wild multiplier on the board.

03 WANTED FREE SPINS

Three, four or five WANTED posters open 10, 12 or 15 free spins.

04 STICKY, GROWING WILDS

In free spins Wilds on reels two to four stick, and gain +1 every time a new Wild lands on their reel.
