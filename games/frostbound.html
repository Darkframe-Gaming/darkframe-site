THE FEATURES

How it plays

A cold start, a long cascade — and a ladder that only climbs.

01 1,024 WAYS · TUMBLE

Wins pay left to right across 1,024 ways. Every winning symbol shatters and the board tumbles, so a single spin can pay again and again.

02 GHOST CAPTAIN WILD

The captain haunts reels two to four, substituting for every paying symbol and joining wins from the middle of the board.

03 FROZEN CHESTS

Chests are never cleared by a tumble. Collect three or more on the board and they crack open 10 free spins.

04 THE DOUBLING LADDER

In free spins one shared ladder doubles on every winning cascade — up to ×4,096 — and multiplies every win the Ghost Captain is part of.
