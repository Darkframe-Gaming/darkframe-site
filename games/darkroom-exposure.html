THE FEATURES

How it plays

Every mechanic below feeds the next.

01 20 LINES · READ BOTH WAYS

Twenty fixed paylines on a 6×4 board, each one evaluated from the leftmost reel and from the rightmost. Three or more from either end pays — every line is effectively two.

02 GLASS-PLATE REGISTRATION

The board is three glass negatives on rails. A win draws its crimson line, the plate beneath it slides one cell, the symbols travel with it and the board is re-read. Symbols that end up aligned across a plate boundary snap into focus and are registered — and in the base game a registration pays on the spot, its symbol's three-of-a-kind value, before the plates move again. The camera registers too: across a rail it stands in for the paying symbol facing it. In the Red Room the marks are banked instead, for the Final Exposure.

03 THE SPIRIT REEL

A single spirit landing anywhere in a reel's window expands over the whole column: a full wild reel with a multiplier badge of ×2, ×3, ×5 or ×8. Wins passing through her are multiplied, several spirit reels multiply together, and her reel is held while the plates slide around it.

04 RED ROOM & FINAL EXPOSURE

Three or more photographs open the Red Room, where the spirit haunts — she stays for the rest of the round — and every win carries the room's multiplier. Six perfect registrations, the same symbol aligned across all three plates, trigger the Final Exposure: the shutter fires and every registered symbol pays again — each bracket paying the symbol it photographed, whatever has slid beneath it since.
