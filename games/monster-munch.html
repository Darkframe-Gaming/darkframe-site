THE FEATURES

How it plays

Every mechanic below feeds the next.

01 1,024 WAYS

Five reels, four rows, no paylines. Matching symbols on adjacent reels pay wherever they land — up to 1,024 ways on every single spin.

02 CASCADE MULTIPLIER

Winners get eaten, the board refills from above, and the chain multiplier climbs with every cascade in the same spin. One lucky board can pay four or five times over before it settles.

03 SPREADING CAVEMAN WILD

When the caveman takes part in a paying way he wakes the winners next to him: those symbols turn into sticky wilds and hold their positions for the rest of the chain, feeding the cascades that follow.

04 SYMBOL MERGE

When a spin stalls with no win at all and one ingredient has piled up eight or more copies on the board, they fuse into the next-tier monster. A dead board cooks itself into a live one.

05 THE MONSTER KITCHEN

Three, four or five golden spatulas open 8, 12 or 16 free spins with the multiplier already at ×2 — and the cascade climb starts from there rather than from one.

06 FOUR WAYS TO BUY IN

Sticky Wild Feast locks every wild that lands. Colossal drops giant 2×2 monsters that permanently raise the feature multiplier. Monster Bonus and Super Bonus simply open the kitchen deeper — 15 spins at ×4, or 20 at ×6.
