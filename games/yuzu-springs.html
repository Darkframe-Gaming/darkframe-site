THE FEATURES

How it plays

Heat builds where the wins land — and in the hidden spring, it stays.

01 CLUSTER PAYS · 5+

Five or more matching symbols touching horizontally or vertically pay as one cluster on a 7 × 7 board. Winning symbols clear and the board tumbles until nothing connects.

02 HEAT MULTIPLIERS

Every square that hosts a win heats up. Hit it again and its multiplier doubles — ×2, ×4, ×8 and on up to ×1,024 — under any cluster that pays across it.

03 STEAM BURST

Squares that reach ×8 boil over: the steam clears the neighbouring symbols, opening the board for the next tumble to land on hot ground.

04 THE HIDDEN SPRING

Three, four or five torii gates open 10, 12 or 15 free spins in the hidden spring, where heat stays on the board for the whole feature. Five buy tiers go up to The Boiling Spring.
