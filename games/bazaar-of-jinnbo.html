THE FEATURES

How it plays

Carpets stack, lamps light, and the total only goes one way.

01 PAY ANYWHERE · 8+

Eight or more matching symbols anywhere on the 6 × 5 board pay. Winners vanish and the board tumbles until nothing pays.

02 FLYING CARPETS

Carpet multipliers from ×2 to ×500 ride in on the tumble. Every carpet on the board is added together and multiplies the sequence win.

03 WISH FREE SPINS

Four or more lamps open 15 free spins; three or more during the round add five more.

04 NEVER-RESET TOTAL

In free spins every carpet feeds a Total Multiplier that never resets — the later the spin, the bigger the wish.
